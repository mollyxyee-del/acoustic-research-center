emize

emize is a romantic inventor & researcher based in brooklyn nyc.

studying the effects of frequencies on human physiology, through both sonic healing and sonic weaponry, as well as herbalism as both medicine and poison. she is currently independently studying archaeacoustics with a focus on the acoustical properties of historical sites of worship. she also traces acoustical development's ties to war and oil...

her conviction is to decentralize sound systems and redistribute sound power, investigate forms of sonic control through sound laws, and study what gets amplified in society... and why.

her inventions have manifested in acoustical tapestries, bespoke electronics like the time machine, and club sound systems, where she creates hi-fi sonic infrastructure for artists.

she is also a spacemaker and cultural worker. the creator of conspire, a QTBIPOC DIY space providing alternative infrastructure for artists in brooklyn. the space is shared with 9 dear friends and artists, who support numerous collectives through space and archival. the loft is a love letter to the infinite manifestations of life affirming and experimental artistic expression, and an experiment to extend past models of capitalism as a worker-owned co-op.

if not immediately clear, she is a capricorn stellium.
